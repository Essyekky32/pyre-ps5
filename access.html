Open PYRE on your PS5

You can reach the hosted page through the PS5's User's Guide browser. A separate browser package is not needed for this first visit.

First visit through the User's Guide

  1. On the PS5, open Settings > Network > Settings > Set Up Internet Connection.
  2. Select your connected network, press Options, and open Advanced Settings.
  3. Set DNS Settings to Manual. Use the community DNS recommended by the Relapse project's own instructions as your Primary DNS:
    Keep a note of your previous DNS settings so you can restore them. This is a community service, rather than a DNS server operated by PYRE.
  4. Open Settings > User's Guide, Health & Safety, and Other Information > User's Guide. The menu may appear as Guide & Tips, Health & Safety, and Other Information.
  5. On the PS5 Jailbreak Redirect page labeled etawen.dev, enter the complete PYRE address in the URL box and choose Go. Open PYRE directly before selecting an exploit on another page:

The website and its hosted payloads are served by the PYRE tool running on your own PC, not from any public site. The community DNS is only an entry point to the browser. That entry service needs to be available for this method to work.

After the Internet Browser package is installed, use its home-screen icon for later visits. The owner confirmed that the browser remains available after a restart. It does not need to be installed each time; the exploit still needs to run after a full restart.
