PYRE / PAYLOAD CATALOG

Your PYRE toolkit

Let Run PYRE finish on its startup page. Then press Circle once for the PYRE menu and choose Additional Payloads. You can also select the Additional Payloads button underneath the completed log.

Run PYRE starts Payload Manager and App Dumper automatically. Their home-screen icons and Open links work once those services are ready. Everything else is listed below: the payloads PYRE already includes, and the tools other developers make.
